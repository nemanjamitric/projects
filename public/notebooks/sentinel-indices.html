Sentinel-2 Spatial Indices

FERI, Advanced Image Processing | Individual course project

I calculated twelve spatial indices from a Sentinel-2 scene. The notebook reads the multispectral bands, resamples 20 m bands to the B08 grid where needed, saves GeoTIFF files, and creates the previews shown here.

Python, NumPy, Rasterio, Matplotlib

Computed results

The results cover vegetation, moisture, water, soil, dry areas, and built-up surfaces. Each image comes from the same source scene but highlights a different feature.
